How your data is handled

In your browser. Reading PDFs and recognizing text in images happen on your device. The file itself is not uploaded for these steps.

AI analysis. Only the extracted text is sent, through a secure server, to the AI provider. You are asked to confirm before each analysis.

Storage. Uploaded documents are not stored permanently.
